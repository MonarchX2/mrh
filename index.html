    About MRH

    Maritime Review Hub · Study workspace

    Overview

    A study and review workspace for maritime learning

    Maritime Review Hub (MRH) is an interactive, responsive study and review platform dedicated to maritime education. It brings together course question decks, practice and review tools, curriculum syllabi, learning modules, academic documents, and secure reviewer materials in one unified workspace.

    Deck Library & Course Navigation

    Explore question decks organized by maritime subject, course, and folder hierarchy. Easily find what you need using keyword search, quick filters, and sort options, with toggleable Grid and List views. Protected folders can be accessed with designated passwords, and your last active deck can be instantly resumed from the home screen.

    Practice and Study Modes

    Master maritime competencies with dedicated learning modes tailored to your study pace:

    • Practice Mode: Test your knowledge with question limits, timed sessions, immediate or end-of-session answer review, and in-depth answer explanations.
    • Study Mode: Engage in active recall flashcard sessions with flexible question navigation, answer reveals, and choice shuffling.
    • Interactive Features: Supports multiple choice, self-rated identification, and cloze questions, alongside question bookmarking/favoriting and ABCD marker toggles.

    Student Portal & Resource Dashboard

    Access structured academic resources across five specialized portal areas:

    • Courses: Comprehensive maritime course catalog and subject references.
    • Syllabus: Official course outlines, competencies, and learning objectives.
    • Modules: Downloadable lecture handouts, reading packages, and lesson guides.
    • Documents: Reference charts, circulars, and supplementary learning files.
    • Reviewers: Dedicated board exam and licensure reviewer materials.

    Filter resources dynamically by Academic Year (1st through 4th Year) and Semester (1st, 2nd, or All Semesters).

    In-App File Previews

    Read and review learning files without leaving the application. MRH features an integrated canvas PDF viewer with responsive controls, zoom, and smooth page navigation on phones, tablets, and desktop computers, as well as native browser playback for supported audio and video assets.

    Reviewer access and security

    Reviewer materials are restricted to approved student and faculty accounts via Google Sign-In. The system verifies enrollment status, coordinates sessions seamlessly across open browser tabs, and delivers protected PDF previews with individualized security watermarks and authorized download capabilities.

    Offline Readiness & Personalization

    Built with offline-first capabilities. Question decks, study progress, bookmarks, and preferences are cached locally on your device for uninterrupted learning when offline, synchronizing automatically whenever an internet connection is established. Personalize your environment with Light and Dark themes, customizable study defaults, and adaptable navigation controls.

    Academic integrity and responsible use

    MRH is an educational aid created to supplement maritime training and examination preparation. Users must only access materials and accounts they are authorized to use. Browser controls cannot prevent screenshots or guarantee that displayed information stays private, so handle protected material responsibly. Always cross-reference procedures and regulations with official maritime administration publications and course instructors.
