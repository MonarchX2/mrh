    About MRH

    A study and review workspace for maritime learning

    Maritime Review Hub (MRH) brings course question decks, practice tools, learning resources, and reviewer materials together in one responsive website.

    Study and practice

    Browse questions by subject, then use Practice or Study sessions to work through them at your own pace. Session settings support features such as answer review, cloze questions, and shuffled questions or choices; available options depend on the deck.

    Student Portal

    Find course materials in the SUBJECTS, MODULES, REVIEWERS, and SYLLABUS areas. Where access is provided, supported files can be previewed in the site, including PDFs on phones and computers.

    Reviewer access

    Reviewer materials are restricted to approved accounts. Protected PDF requests are checked by the service before delivery, and previews may include an identifying watermark.

    Your device and your data

    MRH adapts to small and large screens and saves selected settings and study information in the browser. Deck and account-backed features synchronize when the service is reachable; some previews and account actions require an internet connection.

    Access and responsible use

    Use only materials and accounts you are authorized to access. Browser controls cannot prevent screenshots or guarantee that displayed information stays private, so handle protected material responsibly. MRH is a study aid; consult official course materials and applicable maritime guidance for authoritative information.
